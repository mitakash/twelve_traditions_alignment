A WARNING ABOUT SMALL NUMBERS
With 30 answers in a cell, one answer moves the rate by about 3 points.
With 30 answers in each of two cells, a difference below about 20 points can be chance.
The pilot shows if the test works. It cannot prove that one text is better than another.
